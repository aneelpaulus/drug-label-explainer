This is not medical advice. It shows what the FDA label says, which describes results for groups of people in studies, not what will happen for any one person. Only a doctor who knows the person's full situation can say whether a drug is a good choice. Bring your questions to them.

Where the information comes from. Drug labels come from the FDA through openFDA, the FDA's free public data service. First approval dates come from Drugs@FDA. Key results are pulled out of the label text automatically and can occasionally miss or misplace a number, so check the full text for anything important.

Privacy. Your search goes only to openFDA. If you use the AI button, the label text and your key go only to Anthropic. Nothing is stored. Not affiliated with the FDA.
